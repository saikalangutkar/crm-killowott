The Problem

The WooCommerce-to-CRM Disconnect

Standard WooCommerce stores lose critical customer relationship signals due to fragmentation, guest orders, and static email templates.

1. Guest Checkout Blindspot

Over 40% of WooCommerce purchases occur as guests (customer_id: 0), fracturing customer purchase histories and hiding true Lifetime Value.

2. Static Pipeline Silos

Order status updates remain buried inside store admin panels rather than driving automated, customer-facing omnichannel notification workflows.

3. Zero Autonomous Recovery

When orders are cancelled or high-value VIP whales purchase, store owners receive no actionable synthesis or intelligent recovery campaigns.

Clean Architecture

Explicit Enterprise Design Patterns

Implemented in src/lib/crm-engine.ts to ensure testability, loose coupling, and enterprise scalability.

Adapter Pattern

WooCommerceAdapter

Translates external snake_case schemas and resolves customer_id = 0 by indexing billing.email, merging multi-order guest spend seamlessly.

Strategy Pattern

SegmentationStrategy

ISegmentationStrategy defines swappable evaluation algorithms. Administrators can toggle between Tiered LTV, Frequency, or RFM live in the UI.

Observer Pattern

WorkflowEngine

Decoupled subject notifying CommunicationWorkflowObserver, AiTriageObserver, and analytics monitors on all order transitions.
